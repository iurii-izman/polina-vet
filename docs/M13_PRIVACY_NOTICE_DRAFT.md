# M13 Privacy Notice Draft — owner/legal review required

This draft is intentionally not presented as approved legal text. Before public activation, confirm the responsible controller identity, purpose/legal basis, user rights wording, contact route, retention wording, and the final notice version.

POLINA VET uses information submitted through the inquiry form to receive and manage an operational request, understand the requested context, contact the person through the selected channel, and coordinate an appropriate next step. The form is not an appointment confirmation, emergency chat, or guarantee of an immediate response.

The proposed operational record may include name, contact value, locality, optional address, animal context, short description, language, and campaign attribution. It is stored in a private operational database and accessible only to authorized POLINA VET Office users. A short operational notification may contain only the inquiry reference, context, locality, and Office link; it must not contain contact details or the description.

Standalone closed inquiries are proposed for deletion after 12 months unless a separately approved future record requires a different policy. The form does not accept uploads. No inquiry content is sent to public analytics.

Required gate: replace this draft with an approved, versioned notice and set the corresponding Worker configuration before `PUBLIC_INTAKE_ENABLED=true`.
