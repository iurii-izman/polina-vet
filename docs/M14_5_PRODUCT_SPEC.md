# M14.5 Observability, Product Telemetry & Learning Foundation

M14.5 adds privacy-safe observability around the private Office workflow. It measures system behavior and workflow structure, not people, patients, or clinical outcomes.

## Scope

- Cloudflare-native structured logs and traces.
- Controlled error taxonomy and same-origin mutation protection.
- Typed Workers Analytics Engine workflow telemetry.
- Small client-friction contract with no replay, DOM, keystrokes, or raw errors.
- Aggregate daily learning snapshots from D1.
- Metric definitions and future 30/60-day review templates.

## Explicit exclusions

No public Intake activation, production clinical activation, M14 production migration, AI analytics, clinical outcome analytics, doctor ranking, dashboards, session replay, external observability SaaS, M15 automation, or M16 Clinical Copilot.

Observation period: NOT STARTED until intentional production Office activation.
