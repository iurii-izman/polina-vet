# M12 content growth system

Growth pillars are Pets (symptoms/when to act, prevention, vaccination, parasites, first aid, preparation), Farm (records, youngstock, multiple sick animals, mastitis, prevention), and Shared (observation, transport, first-aid preparedness).

Each approved article can produce a distribution pack: canonical link, Telegram post, Instagram caption, Facebook explanation, short-video hook/outline, card headline, CTA, and campaign source/content code. Workflow: idea → research → draft → Polina medical review → approved article → adaptations → distribution → attribution → measurement. Automation may create templates and links; it may not approve medical claims, publish, reply to DMs, diagnose, or triage.

The 42 M10 drafts remain drafts and are planning inputs only.
