## Audit outcome

The six existing RU articles cover GI observation, vaccination, parasite prevention, symptom records, farm records, and an explicitly synthetic educational case. The fourteen additions fill high-value pet first-response gaps, two shared preparation gaps, and three genuinely farm/group-specific gaps without creating location SEO pages, testimonials, pricing, fake availability, or a second urgent article.

All new RO and UK documents use the RU source as `translatedFrom`, share its `translationGroupId`, and intentionally omit `sourceMedicalRevision` until a human review action confirms the translation. Their expected derived state is `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, not `CURRENT`.
